Poienel — garden and Miyawaki forest in Crucea, Constanța

An oasis of quiet and rich greenery — a weekend retreat in Crucea, Dobrogea, Romania

About the project

Poienel is a garden in the making in Crucea, Constanța County, in northern Dobrogea, Romania — a retreat of quiet and rest in the middle of nature.

Vision

From steppe to oasis: a property turned into rich green space, with 65 species of trees, shrubs and aromatic plants, designed as a weekend retreat — the place to escape routine and reconnect with nature.

The north plan: a Miyawaki forest

A dense, self-regenerating forest — 340 m² with 919 saplings of 30 native Dobrogea forest-steppe species, planted at the natural density of 3 plants/m², which in 5–10 years will create a dense green space and a quiet place to rest.
